## Bad Apple!!

Bad Apple!! is a popular PV made by fans of [Touhou](https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Touhou_Wiki). The song is an arrangement of Lotus Land Story stage 3 theme from Touhou.
The song itself was released by Alstroemeria Records as part of their Lovelight album in 2007. Although official Touhou music made by the game's creator, ZUN, has no lyrics, Alstroemeria Records added 
lyrics and featured the Japanese singer nomico as a vocalist.

One year later, a fan on [Nico Nico Douga](https://www.nicovideo.jp/) uploaded a [video](https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/nm3601701) of a storyboard that would pair well with the song.
In 2009, a Nico Nico user named [Anira](https://www.nicovideo.jp/user/10731211) saw the request and animated the now [famous video](https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm8628149).

Because the animation was monochrome, many fans challenged themselves to recreate the video in any way they could.
This is not limited to animation though; many people have also made their own remixes and covers of the song.
